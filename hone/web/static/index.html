Turn your investment views into a portfolio you can explain.

Hone turns what you believe about a stock, a coin or your own income into disciplined position sizes. Compare a model-proposed allocation with your current one, see why each position changed, and test it with paper money.

How it works

  1. Understand your risk: an adaptive questionnaire — Eight either/or choices between a sure amount and a gamble — estimates your risk aversion, with its uncertainty.
  2. Express your views: a target price, a horizon and a confidence for any asset.
  3. See your allocation: current versus model-proposed weights, with a plain-English reason for every change.
  4. Hedge and test: stress scenarios, tail risk, hedges, and a historical backtest.

Three products, one method

Stocks and Crypto size portfolio positions. Prediction Markets sizes event contracts that hedge income you already depend on.

Is this real money?

No. Hone is an educational research tool. It runs on sample data or an Alpaca paper-trading account, never places orders for you, and is not investment advice.
