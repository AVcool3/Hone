Know what a bad season would cost you.

Hone is for businesses and workers whose income depends on the weather. Tell it what you do, where, and roughly what you bring in. It works out how often a season goes badly where you are, what that would cost you, and what fair protection against it should cost.

Built on records, not guesses

Snow, rain, heat and frost records from the nearest weather station, and what bad seasons have cost ski resorts, park operators, contractors and farms.

Where things stand

Hone prices protection and explains the price. It doesn't sell insurance or place trades. We're opening it to a small group first.
