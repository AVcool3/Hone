Measure your risk aversion

30 quick choices between pairs of lotteries (3 rounds × 10 rows, rising stakes). Pick the one you'd genuinely rather play — there are no right answers. Your choices are run through a maximum-likelihood engine (CRRA utility + noise) to produce your risk-aversion parameter γ and your tier out of 50.
